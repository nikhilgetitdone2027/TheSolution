### MODULE 1: FEEDSTOCK SYNERGY, CONTAMINATION, AND ACID GAS SCRUBBER SIZING
Create/Extend `ml/app/stoichiometry.py` (and export types to frontend):

1. **PVC Dechlorination Chemistry:**
   - For every kilogram of feed with $w_{\text{PVC}}$ weight fraction:
     $$m_{\text{HCl}} = \text{Feed Mass (kg)} \times w_{\text{PVC}} \times \left(\frac{M_{\text{HCl}}}{M_{\text{VCM}}}\right) = \text{Feed Mass} \times w_{\text{PVC}} \times \left(\frac{36.46}{62.50}\right) \approx 0.5834 \times m_{\text{PVC}}$$
   - Calculate neutralizer consumption using hydrated lime $\text{Ca(OH)}_2$:
     $$\text{Ca(OH)}_2 + 2\text{HCl} \rightarrow \text{CaCl}_2 + 2\text{H}_2\text{O}$$
     $$m_{\text{Ca(OH)}_2} = \left(\frac{m_{\text{HCl}}}{2 \times 36.46}\right) \times 74.09 \times \text{Safety Factor (1.20)}$$
   - Generate automated industrial alert:
     * If $w_{\text{PVC}} > 0.5\%$: Trigger `ACID_GAS_CORROSION_RISK` warning and output required $\text{Ca(OH)}_2$ guard bed mass (kg/day).
     * Recommend operational stage: *"Two-stage thermal treatment required: Isothermal pre-dechlorination at 300Â°C prior to main pyrolysis reactor."*

2. **PET Terephthalic Sublimation Risk:**
   - If $w_{\text{PET}} > 3.0\%$: Flag `SOLID_DEPOSITION_RISK` (Sublimation of terephthalic/benzoic acid causes condenser tube fouling and wax polymerization).

3. **Radical Synergy Index:**
   - High polyolefin fraction ($PE + PP > 70\%$) accelerates hydrogen transfer to PS radicals, lowering heavy tar formation. Classify synergy as `HIGH_SYNERGY`, `NEUTRAL`, or `ANTAGONISTIC`.

### MODULE 2: THERMODYNAMICS, HHV & THERMAL AUTARKY (NET ENERGY RATIO)
Implement rigorous energy balances inside `ml/app/thermodynamics.py`:

1. **Elemental Composition & Feedstock HHV:**
   - Standard polymer elemental mass fractions:
     * PE/PP ($\text{C}_2\text{H}_4 / \text{C}_3\text{H}_6$): $C = 85.71\%, H = 14.29\%$
     * PS ($\text{C}_8\text{H}_8$): $C = 92.26\%, H = 7.74\%$
     * PVC ($\text{C}_2\text{H}_3\text{Cl}$): $C = 38.43\%, H = 4.84\%, Cl = 56.73\%$
     * PET ($\text{C}_{10}\text{H}_8\text{O}_4$): $C = 62.50\%, H = 4.17\%, O = 33.33\%$
   - Compute aggregate feed elemental fractions: $C_{\text{blend}}, H_{\text{blend}}, O_{\text{blend}}, Cl_{\text{blend}}$.
   - Compute Higher Heating Value using modified Dulong/Boie correlation:
     $$\text{HHV}_{\text{feed}} (\text{MJ/kg}) = 0.3491\,C + 1.1783\,H - 0.1034\,O - 0.0151\,Cl$$

2. **Reactor Parasitic Heat Demand ($Q_{\text{required}}$):**
   - Sensible heat to raise feed from $T_{\text{ambient}} = 25^\circ\text{C}$ to $T_{\text{reactor}}$ (mean specific heat $c_p \approx 2.1\text{ kJ/kg}\cdot\text{K}$):
     $$Q_{\text{sensible}} = \text{Feed Mass} \times c_p \times (T_{\text{reactor}} - 25)$$
   - Endothermic heat of reaction/cracking ($\Delta H_{\text{rxn}} \approx 1050\text{ kJ/kg}$ for polyolefins).
   - Thermal loss factor (assume 15% convective/radiative casing losses):
     $$Q_{\text{total\_thermal}} = (Q_{\text{sensible}} + \Delta H_{\text{rxn}}) \times 1.15$$

3. **Syngas Energy & Thermal Autarky:**
   - Syngas fraction heating value ($\text{LHV}_{\text{gas}} \approx 32.0\text{ MJ/kg}$ for hydrocarbon cracked syngas).
   - Energy recovered in non-condensable gas:
     $$E_{\text{gas}} = m_{\text{gas}} \times \text{LHV}_{\text{gas}}$$
   - **Autarky Metric:**
     $$\text{Thermal Autarky (\%)} = \min\left(100.0, \frac{E_{\text{gas}}}{Q_{\text{total\_thermal}}} \times 100\right)$$
     $$\text{Net Energy Ratio (NER)} = \frac{E_{\text{oil}} + E_{\text{gas}}}{Q_{\text{total\_thermal}} + E_{\text{electrical\_parasitic}}}$$
   - Report clearly whether the plant can run self-sustained without external natural gas combustion.

### MODULE 3: DOWNSTREAM PYROLYSIS OIL UPGRADING & PONA ESTIMATOR
Implement output oil chemical classification in `ml/app/oil_quality.py`:

1. **Effective $H/C$ Atomic Ratio:**
   $$H/C = \frac{(H_{\text{oil\_wt\%}} / 1.008)}{(C_{\text{oil\_wt\%}} / 12.011)}$$
   * $H/C \ge 1.85$: High paraffinic/olefinic aliphatic cut.
   * $1.60 \le H/C < 1.85$: Mixed synthetic crude.
   * $H/C < 1.60$: Heavy aromatic cut (dominated by PS styrene monomer/dimer/oligomer fractions).

2. **PONA Hydrocarbon Estimate:**
   - **Paraffins & Olefins ($\approx$ PE + PP influence):** Linear carbon chains ($\text{C}_6 - \text{C}_{24}$).
   - **Aromatics ($\approx$ PS influence):** Benzene, toluene, ethylbenzene, styrene.
   - **Naphthenes/Oxygenates ($\approx$ PET/PVC influence):** Polar traces requiring hydrodeoxygenation.

3. **Refinery Upgrading Classification Recommendation:**
   - Emit one of three industrial routing verdicts:
     1. *"Direct Petrochemical Feedstock: Suitable for steam cracker blending up to 10% without hydrotreatment."*
     2. *"Hydroprocessing Required: Saturated dewaxing & de-aromatization needed for EN 590 diesel compliance."*
     3. *"Heavy Industrial Fuel Oil: Sub-spec for transportation; suitable for industrial boilers/furnaces."*

### MODULE 4: 3-WAY COMPARATIVE LIFE CYCLE ASSESSMENT (LCA) DISPLACEMENT ENGINE
Implement environmental displacement math in `ml/app/lca.py`:

Compare 1 Metric Ton (1000 kg) processed across three pathways:

1. **Pathway A (Sanitary Landfill):**
   - Direct fossil $\text{CO}_2$: Low initial emissions ($0.05\text{ kg CO}_2\text{e/kg}$).
   - Environmental Penalty: $1.0\text{ m}^3$ landfill volume consumed; long-term microplastic and phthalate leachate liability.

2. **Pathway B (Waste-to-Energy Incineration):**
   - Stoichiometric combustion of carbon into atmospheric $\text{CO}_2$:
     $$\text{Emissions}_{\text{incineration}} = \text{Feed Mass} \times C_{\text{blend}} \times \left(\frac{44.01}{12.011}\right) \approx 2.8 - 3.1\text{ Tonnes CO}_2\text{e / Ton plastic}$$
   - Grid electricity displacement credit: $-0.65\text{ Tonnes CO}_2\text{e}$ (assuming 25% net thermal-to-electric efficiency at $0.4\text{ kg CO}_2/\text{kWh}$).
   - Net Carbon Footprint: $+2.25\text{ Tonnes CO}_2\text{e / Ton plastic}$.

3. **Pathway C (CHEM2ENERGY Catalyzed Pyrolysis & Circular Recovery):**
   - Direct process thermal footprint: $+0.35\text{ Tonnes CO}_2\text{e}$ (fuel consumed for heating).
   - Virgin naphtha extraction & refining displacement credit:
     $$\text{Credit}_{\text{avoided}} = -1.0 \times m_{\text{oil}} \times 1.75\text{ kg CO}_2\text{e/kg naphtha}$$
   - Net Circular Carbon Impact:
     $$\Delta \text{CO}_2\text{e}_{\text{net}} = \text{Emissions}_{\text{process}} - \text{Credit}_{\text{displaced}}$$
   - Calculate and display: **"Net Carbon Avoided vs Incineration"** ($\text{Tonnes CO}_2\text{e / Day}$).
